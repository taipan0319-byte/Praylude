Week one · Day 1Praying for marriage

Tell God what you want

Father, I want to marry. I want to know someone well, to be known in return, and to build a faithful life together.

Please help me meet someone with whom I can share that life. Give me good judgment when a relationship begins. Keep disappointment from making me bitter, and help me trust You with what I cannot arrange.

Through Christ our Lord. Amen.

Stay a moment with the reflection

Maybe you’ve become good at sounding relaxed about marriage. You tell people you’re seeing what happens. Then you go home from another wedding and wish there were someone to talk to in the car.

You can tell God that. In Matthew’s Gospel, Jesus invites us to ask our Father for good things. If you hope for marriage, bring that hope into prayer plainly.

For today: Finish this sentence privately: “God, what I find hardest to admit about wanting marriage is…”

Faith & real life

For the prayers.
And the first coffees.

Preparation for marriage reaches into ordinary life. Here’s a little of what we’re writing about.

Put something on the calendar

Stay for coffee after Mass. Accept the dinner invitation. Find a parish group you would enjoy attending more than once. Choose one specific social plan this week. An evening can be worthwhile even if you don’t meet anyone you want to date.

Let them finish

During one conversation, put your phone away and let the person finish. Ask a follow-up question before offering your own story or advice. The person you marry will sometimes need that same patience.

Would you like to get coffee?

“I’ve enjoyed talking with you. Would you like to go on a coffee date Saturday?” A simple invitation gives the other person something clear to answer. Give them room to respond freely, and treat their answer with respect.

A few things
you might wonder.

Is Praylude a dating app?

Praylude is a Catholic prayer companion for people hoping for marriage. There are no profiles, matches, or messages between users. We’re bringing together prayer, Church teaching, and thoughtful help with preparing for and pursuing marriage.

Can I try it on my iPhone?

Yes. The early preview opens in your browser, including Safari on your iPhone. It is still in development and includes draft content. A native iPhone app and reliable daily reminders are part of the plan.

Do I have to choose between Prepare and Seek?

The preview lets you begin with “A bit of everything.” You can explore prayers for patient discernment alongside prayers asking God to help you meet a spouse.

Who are the prayers for?

Men and women who hope for a Catholic marriage. You can begin whether prayer is already part of your day or you’re finding your way back to it.
